What is a token, anyway?

A token is a chunk of text — roughly ¾ of a word in English. "hello world" is 2 tokens. We estimate tokens from your text length, so the count is close but not exact. The price check, on the other hand, is live.

Why do replies (output) cost more?

Providers charge more for generated tokens than for tokens you send in. Output tokens usually cost 3–5× as much, which is why a long answer can dominate your bill.

Is this the exact amount I'll be billed?

It's a close estimate from published list prices. Thinking tokens, tool calls, regional price changes and account discounts can change the final invoice. Use the source links to double-check current prices.
